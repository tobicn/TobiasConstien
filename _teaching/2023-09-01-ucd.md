During my PhD Program at the **School of Psychology at University College Dublin**, I have had the chance to lecture, teach, and assist in a number of undergraduate and postgraduate modules. This included previously prepared lectures, but also lectures and content I suggested and prepared myself (e.g., *Play and Development*, PSY20200). As part of these modules, I took on grading of end-of-term written assignments.

**Supervision**

As part of my PhD project, I got to work with and co-supervise undergraduate and postgraduate students along my own supervisor Assoc. Prof. Michelle Downes. Thus far, co-supervision has led to one successful undergraduate thesis and two successful master theses (including one which was published; Kelly et al., [2026](https://tobicn.github.io/TobiasConstien/publication/2026-07-31-not-an-extra)).
