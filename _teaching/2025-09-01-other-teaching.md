Alongside wonderful collaborators from the **European Federation of Dramatherapy**, I developed a Case Study Program for experienced and practising dramatherapists that guided them through a series of monthly workshops as well as individual mentoring to write their first case study based on their work. This was part of a larger effort to foster research capacity within the field of dramatherapy. An evaluation study of our first iteration of this program, which I lead on, was published in the Arts of Psychotherapy (Constien et al., [2026](https://tobicn.github.io/TobiasConstien/publication/2026-09-03-what-works)).
